SENTIMENT THEMES

✖ DOMINANT NEGATIVES

  • Price / Value — #1 complaint. Customers call food "overpriced" vs. portion size & quality. UK customers cite £35–43 for two burgers and a drink.
  • Declining Quality — "Getting worse and worse." Soggy buns, lukewarm fries, small portions, inconsistent meat quality.
  • Slow Service — 20–30+ minute waits for simple orders are frequently cited.
  • Cleanliness — Reports of dirty kitchens, rubbish accumulation, and unsanitary delivery packaging.
  • Staff / Corporate Response — Inattentive or rude staff; complaints about unresponsive corporate channels.

▹ DOMINANT POSITIVES

  • Burger Taste — Fans describe ShackBurger as genuinely good and worth paying more than standard fast food.
  • Shakes / Drinks — Milkshakes consistently praised as "some of the best in the industry."
  • Select Locations — Victoria London, Burlington MA, and Gatwick earn praise for cleanliness & friendly staff.
  • Digital Ordering — Kiosks appreciated for speed and convenience.
  • Gluten-Free Options — Noted positively by multiple reviewers.

NOTABLE CONTROVERSIES
2020
PPP Loan Scandal
Received $10M in pandemic relief intended for small businesses; returned funds after public backlash. Danny Meyer's USHG separately took $11–27M.
2020
NYPD “Poisoning” Hoax
Police unions falsely accused employees of poisoning officers' milkshakes. Investigation cleared Shake Shack; manager later sued NYPD for defamation.
2023–25
Animal Welfare Criticism
Identified by The Humane League for failing to report cage-free egg progress; 0% compliance on chicken genetic/slaughter standards as of 2025.
2024
D.C. Assault Allegation
Gay man alleged assault by employees after kissing his boyfriend at a D.C. location.
REGIONAL SENTIMENT ANALYSIS

A stark divide exists between markets. International locations (Dubai, Bangkok, Singapore, Macau) average 3.59/5 with mostly positive reviews. The UK — Shake Shack's largest Trustpilot reviewer base — scores 2.23/5, dragged down by London tourist-trap branches. The US scores worst at 1.88/5, with only 15% of US reviews being positive.

London Branch Breakdown

Within London, sentiment varies wildly by branch. Gatwick Airport (4.75/5) and Canary Wharf (3.86/5) receive glowing reviews — notably for a standout employee named Zuber who appears in multiple 5-star reviews. Meanwhile, Covent Garden, Camden, and Leicester Square all average 1.0/5, citing dirty conditions, rude staff, and extreme overpricing in tourist areas.

SENTIMENT OVER TIME

Sentiment has been volatile. The 2020 PPP loan scandal cratered trust (avg 1.90/5) with reviewers calling Shake Shack "thieves." A gradual recovery peaked in 2024 (2.64/5), but 2025 shows the sharpest decline yet at 1.71/5 — the worst year on record — driven by aggressive UK pricing and declining US quality.
